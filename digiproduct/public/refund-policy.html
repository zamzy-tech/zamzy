Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Digital Products

Due to the nature of digital products, all sales are generally considered final once digital access has been granted and products have been downloaded.

Exceptions

Refunds may be considered in the following cases:

  • Technical issues preventing product access that cannot be resolved by support
  • Product delivered does not match the description at the time of purchase
  • Duplicate payment due to system error

How to Request a Refund

Contact us at work@zamzy.in with your order number and reason for the refund request. Refund requests will be reviewed within 3-5 business days.

Refund Processing

If approved, refunds will be processed back to the original payment method. Previously downloaded files cannot be remotely recovered after a refund.

Access After Refund

Upon refund, access to the purchased products will be revoked and future downloads will be blocked.
