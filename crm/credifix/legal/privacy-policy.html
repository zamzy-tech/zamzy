Privacy Policy

CREDIFIX regulatory compliance policies and disclosures.

At CREDIFIX, accessible from https://credifix.in, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by CREDIFIX and how we use it.

1. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your personal information.

When you register for a Loan or Taxation Enquiry through our WhatsApp OTP verification forms, we collect your name, phone number, and loan requirements. This data is verified live using our WhatsApp Gateway API.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and loan advisory desks.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our services.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website.
  • Contact you for loan processing and document curation.
  • Send live WhatsApp verification codes (OTPs).

4. Data Security

We implement robust encryption and security procedures to ensure that your verified contact details and property documents are safe from unauthorized access. Your data is only shared with approved banking underwriter panels after your explicit validation.
