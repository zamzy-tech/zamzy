Corporate Profile

Lending Advisory Backed by Three Decades of Experience

CREDIFIX is a premier financial consultancy operating out of Madhapur, Hyderabad. We specialize in structuring retail loans, commercial MSME credit, and taxation services.

Rather than acting as a direct lender, we leverage direct code access across 100+ partnering banks, housing finance companies (HFCs), and NBFCs. This allows us to advocate for borrowers, optimize processing fees, and negotiate the best possible ROI, even for complex profiles including candidates seeking private funding or low CIBIL loan assistance.

Our Core Commitments

  • Borrower Advocacy

    We negotiate directly with bank underwriters to ensure files are presented optimally.

  • Absolute Privacy

    No corporate spam. Your data stays encrypted and is routed only to approved bank panels.

  • End-to-End Assistance

    From document curation to registration and final disbursement, we handle the desk work.
