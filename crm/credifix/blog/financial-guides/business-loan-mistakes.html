Common Business Loan Mistakes to Avoid: Underwriting Tips

Written by: Sai Krishna J

Filing a commercial loan application requires absolute accuracy. Even minor errors in documentation, wrong product selection, or undisclosed liabilities can lead to immediate rejections. This guide highlights the most common business loan mistakes promoters make and how to avoid them.

1. Over-Borrowing and Ignoring Debt Capacity

A common mistake is applying for the maximum loan limit without evaluating daily cash flow capacity. Excessive monthly EMI obligations can strain operational liquidity, leading to payment defaults. Always align your borrowing with a clear capital deployment plan and verify your debt-service capacity before applying.

2. Critical Underwriting Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these key errors during the application process:

  • Undisclosed Liabilities: Hiding active credit card debt or personal loans from underwriters leads to rejection. Lenders verify all active liabilities through credit bureau checks.
  • Too Many Simultaneous Applications: Submitting multiple formal loan applications in a short period triggers multiple hard credit inquiries, lowering your credit score.
  • Poor Current Account Management: Frequent cheque bounce records, high cash withdrawals, or low average balances in your business current accounts signal financial stress to underwriters.

3. Keep Business Compliance Updated

Ensure that all municipal trade licenses, GST filings, and MSME registrations are active and match the address details in your loan application to prevent administrative delays.
